Privacy Policy

Sixtyfold · Effective November 18, 2026

Sixtyfold has no account, no servers that receive your data, no ads and no third-party analytics. Your habits, ticks, goals and scores stay on your iPhone.

In short

  • Account: none — there is no sign-up.
  • Data we collect on our servers: none.
  • Analytics, ads, tracking: none. If you installed Sixtyfold from an ad, Apple's AdAttributionKit / SKAdNetwork may send the ad network one number (0–63) for how far you got, without any identifier; we do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • AI providers: none — the app does not send your content to any AI service.
  • App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.

Who we are

Sixtyfold is published by Valeria Pastukhova (“we”). Contact: gordenigill@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The following stays on your iPhone, in the app’s own storage. We never receive it.

  • Your challenge, habits, daily ticks, goals and quiz answers
  • A small summary for the widgets (day number, streak, today's count, focus score, your first goal's title and progress), shared only with Sixtyfold's own widgets on the same iPhone
  • A funnel counter (quiz steps, plan, paywall, trial, days completed) used to compute the ad-attribution value
  • Whether Sixtyfold Pro is active

Deleting the app deletes this data.

Permissions the app asks for

Each one is optional. You can change your choice any time in iPhone Settings → Sixtyfold.

  • Notifications. Asked once your 60-day plan is ready. Used for local reminders you control in Settings → Reminders: a morning note with today's habits, an evening note only on days you haven't finished, a note after two days away, and one reminder a day before a free trial ends. All are scheduled on your iPhone; no notification server is involved and no device token leaves the phone.

Services provided by others

  • Apple — AdAttributionKit / SKAdNetwork — if you installed after tapping an ad, Apple may send the ad network a single number from 0 to 63 that says how far you got (for example "started a trial"), after a random delay, with no identifier for you or your device and nothing about your habits. (their privacy policy)
  • Apple — App Store — processes subscriptions and purchases. We receive no payment details and do not learn who you are. (their privacy policy)

Children

Sixtyfold is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. If you contact us by email, we use your message only to answer it and delete it when the conversation is over. Residents of California and other US states with privacy laws: we do not sell or share personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will be noted in the app’s release notes.
