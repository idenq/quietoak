Frequently asked questions

What's free?

The 60-day challenge, 3 daily habits, 1 goal, and the Challenge and Year widgets. Sixtyfold Pro adds unlimited habits and goals, life areas and the Today and Goal Pace widgets.

How do I add the widgets?

Long-press the Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit or Customize, then Add Widget and choose Sixtyfold. The Widgets tab in the app shows every widget with your data.

Will I be reminded before the trial ends?

Yes, if you allow notifications when you start the trial: one local reminder two days before the first charge. The paywall shows the exact charge date before you start.

What happens if I miss a day?

The challenge keeps counting. Your streak counts days where every habit was done, and it only breaks once the day is actually over. The 60-day map marks each day as done, partly done or missed.

Can I start over?

Yes. Settings → Start a new 60 days clears your ticks and starts again from day 1.

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no sync. Deleting the app deletes everything.

Subscriptions

  • Restore purchases: open the app → Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • Cancel: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Sixtyfold Pro → Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Sixtyfold has no account, no servers that receive your data, no ads and no third-party analytics. Your habits, ticks, goals and scores stay on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.
