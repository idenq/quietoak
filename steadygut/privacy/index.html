Privacy Policy

Steadygut · Effective November 18, 2026

Steadygut has no account, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your diary is stored on your iPhone and leaves it only when you share a PDF or CSV yourself.

In short

  • Account: none — there is no sign-up.
  • Data we collect on our servers: none.
  • Analytics, ads, tracking: none.
  • AI providers: none — the app does not send your content to any AI service.
  • App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.

Who we are

Steadygut is published by Valeria Pastukhova (“we”). Contact: gordenigill@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The following stays on your iPhone, in the app’s own storage. We never receive it.

  • Diary entries: bowel movements (Bristol type, urgency, blood noted), symptoms and severity, meals and tags, medications and doses, sleep hours, stress level, notes
  • Your saved medications and reminder times
  • Settings such as the daily check-in time

Deleting the app deletes this data.

Permissions the app asks for

Each one is optional. You can change your choice any time in iPhone Settings → Steadygut.

  • Notifications. Optional local reminders you turn on: a daily check-in, medication reminders, and a reminder 2 days before a free trial ends. Scheduled on your iPhone.

Services provided by others

  • Apple — App Store — processes subscriptions and purchases. We receive no payment details and do not learn who you are. (their privacy policy)

Health information

Steadygut is a personal diary, not a medical device, and not medical advice. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition and does not recommend diets or medication. Patterns it shows are correlations in your own entries, not causes. See a doctor promptly for red-flag symptoms such as blood in stool, unexplained weight loss, fever, symptoms that wake you at night or severe pain, and talk to your doctor before changing your diet or treatment. Sources: Bristol Stool Form Scale (Lewis & Heaton, Scand J Gastroenterol 1997); red-flag symptoms (NICE guideline CG61; ACG Clinical Guideline on IBS, 2021).

Children

Steadygut is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. If you contact us by email, we use your message only to answer it and delete it when the conversation is over. Residents of California and other US states with privacy laws: we do not sell or share personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will be noted in the app’s release notes.
